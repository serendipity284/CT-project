# Exercise 3 – Individual Proposal for the Course Project

## Project Title

**Smart AI Sleep & Circadian Rhythm Assistant – An AI-Powered Website for Sleep Habit Analysis and Personalized Sleep Scheduling**

## 1. Problem Statement

Sleep is essential for maintaining physical health, mental well-being, and daily productivity. However, many students and young adults have irregular sleep schedules due to academic workloads, late-night screen usage, and changing daily routines. These habits may affect sleep quality, daytime alertness, and the consistency of their sleep-wake cycles.

Many people have difficulty understanding their sleep patterns and developing a consistent sleep schedule that fits their daily responsibilities. Therefore, an AI-powered website is needed to help users analyze their sleep habits, create personalized sleep schedules, and receive practical recommendations for improving their sleep routines.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Smart AI Sleep & Circadian Rhythm Assistant is an AI-powered website that helps users understand and improve their sleep habits through personalized analysis, sleep scheduling, and interactive guidance. It combines rule-based algorithms, sleep-related information retrieval, and AI-powered recommendations to support better sleep management.

Users can record their sleep and wake-up times, analyze their sleep patterns, receive personalized sleep schedule suggestions based on their daily commitments, and ask questions through an AI chatbot. The system can also track progress over time and adjust its recommendations based on users' actual sleep records and feedback.

## 3. Target Users

- **University students:** To manage irregular sleep schedules caused by academic workloads, examinations, and changing class timetables.
- **Young adults:** To develop more consistent sleep habits and improve their daily routines.
- **People with irregular sleep schedules:** To understand their sleep patterns and plan more consistent sleep and wake-up times.

## 4. Key Features

**4.1. Sleep Pattern Analyzer**

Analyzes users' sleep records to calculate sleep duration, identify variations in bedtime and wake-up time, and detect patterns of insufficient sleep relative to their goals. The system presents the results through charts and summaries to help users understand their sleeping habits.

**4.2. Personalized Sleep Schedule Planner**

Generates suggested sleep and wake-up schedules based on users' daily commitments, preferred sleeping times, and target sleep duration. A rule-based scheduling algorithm checks time constraints and helps users develop a more consistent routine. When necessary, the system can suggest gradual schedule adjustments rather than unrealistic immediate changes.

**4.3. AI Sleep Assistant**

Provides an AI chatbot that answers general questions about sleep habits and circadian rhythms, explains sleep analysis results, and offers personalized recommendations based on users' recorded information. The assistant can retrieve relevant sleep-related educational resources to support its responses. It provides general wellness guidance rather than medical diagnosis or treatment.

**4.4. Sleep Progress Tracking and Adaptive Recommendations**

Tracks users' actual sleep schedules and compares them with their planned schedules. The system identifies differences between planned and actual sleep times, monitors progress over time, and updates future recommendations based on recorded behavior and user feedback.

## 5. Proposed Technologies

- **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript:** To build the website interface, interactive dashboards, and sleep-tracking features.
- **Python and FastAPI:** To develop the backend, process user requests, and implement sleep analysis and scheduling algorithms.
- **AI API:** To power the AI chatbot, interpret natural-language questions, and generate explanations and recommendations.
- **Database:** To store sleep records, user preferences, schedules, and learning resources.
- **Data Visualization Library:** To display sleep duration, bedtime consistency, and progress over time through charts.
- **Git and GitHub:** To manage source code, collaborate with team members, and track project development.

## 6. Expected Outcomes

The project aims to deliver a functional website that can analyze sleep records, generate personalized sleep schedules, answer sleep-related questions, and track users' progress over time.

It will help users better understand their sleep habits, develop more consistent routines, and make informed decisions about their daily schedules. The project will also demonstrate the integration of deterministic algorithms, information retrieval, AI capabilities, and user feedback within a unified intelligent system. Through this project, I aim to develop my skills in web development, algorithm design, AI integration, database management, and software testing.
