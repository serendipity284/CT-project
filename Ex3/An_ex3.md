Smart AI Fishing Assistant

1. Project Overview

The project proposes a Smart AI Fishing Assistant designed to help fishing enthusiasts identify local fish species, determine suitable fishing conditions, and learn more about the fish they catch. The assistant provides information about fish commonly found in nearby areas and analyzes current weather conditions to advise users on whether it is a good time to go fishing.

Additionally, users can upload pictures or provide descriptions of their catches to identify fish species, check whether they are legally allowed to catch them, and learn about their current market prices. By bringing these features together, the assistant aims to make fishing easier, more convenient, and more enjoyable.

2. Key Features

* Local Fish Identification: Helps users discover fish species commonly found in their chosen fishing locations, allowing them to understand what types of fish they may encounter.
* Weather Analysis & Fishing Recommendations: Provides weather information and evaluates current conditions to advise users on whether it is a suitable time to fish, helping them plan their fishing trips more effectively.
* Fish Catch Identification: Allows users to upload pictures or describe the fish they have caught to identify its species and learn more about it.
* Fishing Regulations Checker: Informs users whether a particular fish species can legally be caught or kept in their fishing location, including any relevant restrictions or protected-species rules.
* Fish Market Price Information: Provides the estimated current market price of identified fish species, helping users understand the approximate value of their catches.

3. Target Audience

The assistant is designed for fishing enthusiasts, including beginners and experienced anglers, who want to find suitable fishing conditions, identify fish species, and learn about fishing regulations and market prices.

4. Value Proposition & Key Differentiators

By combining local fish information, weather-based fishing recommendations, fish identification, fishing regulations, and market price information in one assistant, the project provides a convenient way for users to plan fishing trips and learn more about their catches.

Unlike applications that focus on only one aspect of fishing, this assistant supports users before and after their fishing trips, helping them decide when to fish, understand what they catch, and determine whether their catches can legally be kept.

5. Expected Impact

The project aims to make fishing more accessible and enjoyable by helping users make informed decisions about fishing locations, weather conditions, and fish species. It also encourages responsible fishing by providing information about legal restrictions and protected species, while offering useful insights into the market value of different catches.
