# Exercise 3 – Individual Proposal for the Course Project

## Project Title

**AI Lecture-to-Notes Converter**

## 1. Problem Statement

University students often need to study large amounts of information from lecture slides and PDF documents. Manually summarizing these materials and organizing important information into study notes can be time-consuming.

An AI-powered system could help students process lecture materials more efficiently by generating structured summaries and highlighting important concepts, definitions, and formulas.

## 2. Target Users

- **University students:** Students who need to review lecture materials from different subjects.
- **Students preparing for examinations:** Students who want to summarize large amounts of information into concise study notes.
- **Students who prefer organized learning materials:** Students who want to review key concepts without repeatedly reading entire documents.

## 3. Project Scope

### Included

- **Document Processing:** Extract text from supported PDF files and lecture slides.
- **Content Summarization:** Generate concise summaries while preserving the main ideas.
- **Key Information Extraction:** Identify important concepts, definitions, formulas, and technical terms.
- **Structured Notes Generation:** Organize summaries into headings, sections, and bullet points.
- **Word Export:** Allow users to export generated notes as Microsoft Word (.docx) documents for editing and future review.

### Excluded

- Answering general university-related questions.
- Creating personalized study schedules.
- Recording and transcribing live lectures.
- Automatically grading assignments or examinations.
- Replacing the original lecture materials or textbooks.

## 4. Key Features

- **Document Upload:** Users can upload supported lecture documents.
- **AI Summarization:** Generate concise summaries of lecture content.
- **Key Concept Extraction:** Highlight important definitions, formulas, and concepts.
- **Organized Study Notes:** Divide content into clear sections for easier reading.
- **Word Export:** Export and edit generated notes in Microsoft Word format.

## 5. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a working prototype that converts lecture documents into concise, structured, and reusable study notes in Microsoft Word format.

The system should help students save time when reviewing lecture materials, identify important information more easily, and organize their learning resources effectively. The quality of the generated notes can be evaluated based on accuracy, completeness, readability, and preservation of essential information from the original documents.
