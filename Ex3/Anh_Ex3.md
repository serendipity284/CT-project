# Exercise 3 – Individual Proposal for the Course Project

## Project Title

**PhishGuard – An AI-Powered Website for Online Scam Detection and Prevention**

## 1. Problem Statement

Online scams are becoming increasingly common as people use the Internet for communication, shopping, and financial transactions. Scammers often use phishing emails, fake websites, fraudulent messages, and malicious links to steal personal information and deceive users.

Many people, especially students and inexperienced Internet users, have difficulty identifying these threats. Therefore, an AI-powered website is needed to help users analyze suspicious content, understand potential risks, and learn how to protect themselves online.

## 2. Proposed Solution

PhishGuard is an AI-powered educational website that helps users recognize and prevent online scams. It combines AI-based analysis with interactive learning to explain suspicious activities and provide practical safety recommendations.

Users can submit suspicious messages or URLs for preliminary analysis, ask cybersecurity questions through an AI chatbot, and practice identifying scams through simulated scenarios and quizzes.

## 3. Target Users

- **Students:** To improve their cybersecurity awareness.
- **General Internet users:** To identify suspicious messages, emails, and links.
- **Inexperienced technology users:** To learn how to recognize scams and protect personal information.

## 4. Key Features

**4.1. AI Scam Message Analyzer**

Analyzes suspicious messages and emails to identify potential warning signs, such as urgent requests, impersonation, and requests for sensitive information. AI explains the risks and suggests safe actions.

**4.2. Suspicious URL Checker**

Examines URLs for suspicious domain names, misleading spelling, and other warning signs. The system can integrate a URL reputation service to improve its assessment. Results are preliminary and cannot guarantee that a website is safe or fraudulent.

**4.3. AI Cybersecurity Assistant**

Provides an AI chatbot that answers questions about online scams, explains suspicious activities, and recommends appropriate safety measures.

**4.4. Phishing Simulation and Interactive Quiz**

Offers simulated scam scenarios and quizzes to help users practice identifying threats, test their knowledge, and understand common scam techniques.

## 5. Proposed Technologies

- **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript:** To build the website interface and interactive features.
- **Python and FastAPI:** To develop the backend and process requests.
- **AI API:** To analyze suspicious messages and power the cybersecurity chatbot.
- **URL Reputation API:** To check URLs against available threat intelligence data.
- **Database or Local Storage:** To store educational content and learning progress.
- **Git and GitHub:** To manage source code and track project development.

## 6. Expected Outcomes

The project aims to deliver a functional website that can analyze suspicious messages, examine potentially dangerous URLs, answer cybersecurity questions, and provide interactive learning activities.

It will help users improve their ability to recognize online scams, make safer decisions, and protect their personal information. The project will also develop my skills in web development, API integration, AI application, and software testing.
