## 2. Team Goals and Expectations
* **Project goal 1:** Create a virtual assistant that can support daily university tasks, such as study scheduling and answering questions about school regulations.
* **Project goal 2:** Apply computational thinking (understand → decompose → represent → decide → execute → verify → improve) to turn ambiguous student requests into a reliable, well-tested process, focusing on sound judgment and evidence rather than model complexity.
* **Target grade / quality level:** Target grade: 9/10. All core features work correctly, code follows the team’s standards, and documents are complete and submitted on time.
* **Effort commitment:** Every member commits full effort (100%) to the project and agrees to carry an equal share of the workload.

## 4. Communication

## 5. Meetings
* **Frequency:** At least one time per week.
* **Time and place:** Preference on Google Meet / place will be announced in advance 24 hours in special cases.
* **Agenda:** The team leader prepares a short agenda and shares it before each meeting.
* **Meeting notes:** One member records the main decisions and assigned tasks and shares them with the group after the meeting.
* **Punctuality:** A member is considered late if they arrive more than 15 minutes after the start of an online meeting, or more than 30 minutes after the start of an in-person meeting.
* **Absence:** A member who cannot attend must notify the team in advance.

---

## 6. Work Process and Standards
* **Task assignment and tracking tool:** GitHub and Notion.
* **Internal deadlines:** Each task is given a clear deadline when it is assigned; members finish their work on time and do not leave it until the last moment.
* **Git workflow:** Branching, commit messages, code review.
* **Quality standards:** Code and documents must be complete, clear, and checked by at least one other member before submission.

---

## 7. Decision Making
Team decisions are made by majority vote. If the vote is tied, the team leader makes the final decision.

---

## 8. Conflict Resolution
When a disagreement arises, the steps are:
1. The members involved discuss the issue directly to find a solution.
2. If the issue cannot be solved, the team leader asks the instructor for help.

## 9. Accountability and Consequences

### Violations and how they are handled
| Violation | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Arriving late to a meeting (more than 15 minutes online or 30 minutes in person) | Reminder and the member sings a song for the team. From the 3rd violation, points are deducted. |
| Refusing to carry out an assigned task | Reminder and point deduction. |
| Finishing a task late or not working on it seriously | Reminder, and the member must help with additional tasks. |
| Missing 2 consecutive meetings without permission | The team reassigns the member's tasks and points are deducted. |

### Contribution and rewards
| Contribution | Recognition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Completing all tasks assigned by the team well | Public praise. |
| Helping another member finish their work, or giving good feedback and ideas | Recognition, and priority in choosing preferred tasks next time. |
| Contributing an excellent idea that supports the team's success | Recognition and bonus points. |

* **Evaluation of contribution:** Each member's contribution is evaluated by the team based on completed tasks, deadlines met, meeting attendance, and support given to teammates.

## 10. Academic Integrity and AI Use
* All work submitted must be the team's own. Copying from other teams, previous students, or any source without permission is plagiarism and is not allowed.
* Generative AI tools may be used for support such as brainstorming, explaining concepts, or debugging, but not to replace a member's own understanding and work. Each member must be able to explain every part of the code they submit.
* Any use of AI tools and any external source (code, documents, images, libraries) must be acknowledged in the project report or code comments.
* All course and instructor rules on academic integrity take priority over this contract.
