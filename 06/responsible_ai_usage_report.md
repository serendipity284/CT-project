# Exercise 5 - Responsible AI Usage

## 1. Have you used AI before?

Yes, I have frequently integrated AI into my daily learning and technical workflow. I regularly utilize AI across several key areas:

* **LLMs & Intelligent Assistants (Gemini, ChatGPT):** Assisting with information retrieval, explaining algorithms, brainstorming ideas, drafting documents, and reviewing/debugging code.
* **Computer Vision & OCR Tools (Tesseract, OpenCV):** Analyzing and extracting data from images and digital documents.
* **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Systems:** Researching and building intelligent AI virtual assistant models for domain-specific knowledge retrieval.

By treating AI as an assistive tool rather than a replacement, I leverage it to augment my technical capabilities, optimize productivity, and accelerate problem-solving.

## 2. Do you have any experience or tips for using AI?

Through my practical usage, I have developed several effective strategies to use AI responsibly, efficiently, and accurately:

* **Create Custom Skills/Prompts for Repetitive Tasks:** For recurring workloads—such as generating specific image styles or formatting plan templates—I design reusable "skills" or structured instructions. This ensures consistency across outputs while saving time.

* **Provide Detailed Context and Clear Requirements:** Giving precise details and setting clear context helps the model deliver accurate outputs on the first attempt, significantly minimizing misunderstandings and reducing token consumption.

* **Enforce Academic Integrity and Citation:** When conducting academic research or factual inquiries, I explicitly instruct the AI to cite verifiable sources. This practice allows me to fact-check generated responses, prevent hallucinations, and ensure academic integrity.

* **Take Ultimate Responsibility for AI Output:** Grounded in Responsible AI principles, I always review and refine AI-generated content before final implementation, remaining accountable for the accuracy and quality of the final work.
